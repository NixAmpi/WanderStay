Help

Answers to common questions about finding and choosing a stay on WanderStay.

Searching for stays

How do I search for a place to stay?

On the homepage, enter a destination, choose your check-in and check-out dates, and select the number of adults and children. Then select Search stays. You can change your search at any time from the bar at the top of the results page.

Why can't I pick my check-out date?

Check-out must be at least one night after check-in, and check-in can't be in the past. Choose your check-in date first, and the calendar will only offer valid check-out dates.

Which destinations can I search?

WanderStay currently lists stays in Boracay, Tagaytay, General Trias (Cavite), La Union, Siquijor, Baguio and Lucban. Start typing in the location field to see suggestions, or browse destinations.

What do my travel dates change in the results?

Results are matched by destination. When you add dates, each stay shows the total price for the number of nights you selected alongside its nightly rate.

Prices and stays

What currency are prices shown in?

Prices are shown in Philippine pesos (₱) per night.

How do I see more photos of a stay?

Open a stay from the results or homepage, then select any of the smaller photos next to the main image to view it larger.

Your account

How do I log in or create an account?

Select Log in or Sign up in the top navigation, or go to the log in or sign up page. You can continue with your phone number, Google, Apple or Facebook.

How do I change my language or currency?

Select the globe icon in the navigation, or open Settings, then choose the Language or Currency tab.
